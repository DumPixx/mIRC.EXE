Story:
IRC-chat was popular at 90s years. But, when ICQ've been released, IRC is not popular anymore. Today 2005's year. Everyone thinks, that IRC - is old bullshit, and WhatsApp - is best. But not for you. Your mission: Check IRC for demons. Since some people think that after the IRC was abandoned, he got a lot of spirits. Somebody thinks, that now, IRC is a part of a DarkNet. 
You're remember, that you've chatted with everyone here, when you were 9 years old. So, next steps of story will be continued by you.
